# Brief 7: Long-form article

**Deliverable:** a single self-contained `index.html` in the workspace root.

**Hard constraints (apply to every brief):**
- One HTML file. No build step. No CDN, no external fonts, no external images, no JS libraries.
- System font stack only. Any imagery must be CSS-only (gradients, shapes, SVG you write inline).
- Must be usable at 375px and 1440px wide. No horizontal scrolling at 375px.
- All copy below must appear verbatim. Do not invent testimonials, logos, statistics or prices.
- No lorem ipsum. No placeholder text.

---

**Goal:** an article page for a piece of writing. Reading comfort is the whole point.

**Required:**
1. Article header: category label, title, standfirst, author line with date.
2. Body with at least six paragraphs and two subheadings.
3. A pull quote.
4. A footnote-style aside.
5. A "related reading" block with three links.

**Fixed copy:**
- Category: "Craft"
- Title: "The cost of a rounded corner"
- Standfirst: "Small decisions about shape carry more meaning than most teams admit."
- Author: "By Dana Whitfield · 14 March 2026"
- Subheadings: "What a radius communicates" and "When to break your own rule"
- Pull quote: "A corner is a promise about how hard the object will be to hold."

**Tone:** editorial. Measure (line length) must be comfortable at 1440px.

---

**When you are done:** write `index.html` and nothing else. Do not explain your choices in the file.
