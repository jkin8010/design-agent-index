# Brief 12: Portfolio case study

**Deliverable:** a single self-contained `index.html` in the workspace root.

**Hard constraints (apply to every brief):**
- One HTML file. No build step. No CDN, no external fonts, no external images, no JS libraries.
- System font stack only. Any imagery must be CSS-only (gradients, shapes, SVG you write inline).
- Must be usable at 375px and 1440px wide. No horizontal scrolling at 375px.
- All copy below must appear verbatim. Do not invent testimonials, logos, statistics or prices.
- No lorem ipsum. No placeholder text.

---

**Goal:** a case-study page for a design consultancy engagement.

**Required:**
1. Title block: client sector, engagement length, role.
2. A "problem" section and an "approach" section, two paragraphs each.
3. Three outcome metrics presented prominently.
4. A process strip with four phases.
5. A closing quote from the client.

**Fixed copy:**
- Title: "Rebuilding a booking flow that lost 40% of users"
- Meta: "Travel · 14 weeks · Product design lead"
- Metrics: "−62% drop-off", "2.4× completed bookings", "11 days to first release"
- Phases: "Audit", "Prototype", "Test", "Ship"
- Quote: "They found the problem in week one and spent the rest of the time fixing it."

**Tone:** evidence-led, no bragging.

---

**When you are done:** write `index.html` and nothing else. Do not explain your choices in the file.
