# Brief 6: Product detail page

**Deliverable:** a single self-contained `index.html` in the workspace root.

**Hard constraints (apply to every brief):**
- One HTML file. No build step. No CDN, no external fonts, no external images, no JS libraries.
- System font stack only. Any imagery must be CSS-only (gradients, shapes, SVG you write inline).
- Must be usable at 375px and 1440px wide. No horizontal scrolling at 375px.
- All copy below must appear verbatim. Do not invent testimonials, logos, statistics or prices.
- No lorem ipsum. No placeholder text.

---

**Goal:** a product page for a desk lamp sold direct-to-consumer.

**Required:**
1. Gallery area on the left (CSS-only product representation), purchase panel on the right.
2. Product title, price, three variant swatches (Charcoal, Sand, Forest), quantity stepper, "Add to cart" button.
3. Three accordion rows: "Materials", "Shipping", "Returns".
4. A four-item spec list.

**Fixed copy:**
- Title: "Fold Task Lamp"
- Price: "$189"
- Blurb: "A counterweighted arm lamp with a 2700K–5000K dimmable head. Aluminium body, weighted base, no exposed wiring."
- Specs: "Height 42cm", "Reach 58cm", "Head 12cm", "Cable 2m"

**Tone:** restrained, material-led. No urgency devices, no countdown timers.

---

**When you are done:** write `index.html` and nothing else. Do not explain your choices in the file.
