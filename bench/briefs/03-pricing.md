# Brief 3: Pricing page

**Deliverable:** a single self-contained `index.html` in the workspace root.

**Hard constraints (apply to every brief):**
- One HTML file. No build step. No CDN, no external fonts, no external images, no JS libraries.
- System font stack only. Any imagery must be CSS-only (gradients, shapes, SVG you write inline).
- Must be usable at 375px and 1440px wide. No horizontal scrolling at 375px.
- All copy below must appear verbatim. Do not invent testimonials, logos, statistics or prices.
- No lorem ipsum. No placeholder text.

---

**Goal:** a pricing page for "Anchor", a website-monitoring service.

**Sections:**
1. Page header with a monthly/annual toggle (visual only, no JS required).
2. Three plan cards side by side, collapsing to one column at 375px.
3. A four-row comparison table.
4. Three-question FAQ.

**Fixed copy:**
- Plans: "Hobby $0 / month — 1 monitor, 5-minute checks, email alerts"; "Team $29 / month — 20 monitors, 1-minute checks, Slack alerts, 12-month history"; "Business $99 / month — 100 monitors, 30-second checks, on-call routing, 24-month history".
- Highlight the Team plan.
- Comparison rows: Checks per month, History, Alert channels, Team members.
- FAQ questions: "Can I change plans later?", "What counts as a monitor?", "Do you offer invoicing?"

**Tone:** matter-of-fact. No "most popular" badge unless you design it deliberately.

---

**When you are done:** write `index.html` and nothing else. Do not explain your choices in the file.
