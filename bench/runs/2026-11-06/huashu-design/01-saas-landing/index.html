What it does

Three jobs, handled.

Auto-categorisation

Every transaction is sorted into the right category the moment it lands, using the chart of accounts you already maintain. No rule lists to write, no spreadsheets to keep in step.

Duplicate detection

Ledgerly compares each charge with the rest of the month and brings likely duplicates to your attention before they reach the ledger. Review the match once; the extra charge stays out of the close.

Month-end close

Your books stay current through the month, so the close becomes a review rather than a rebuild. Export a clean, categorised file to your accounting system when you are ready.
