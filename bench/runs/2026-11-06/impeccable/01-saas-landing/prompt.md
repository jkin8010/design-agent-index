# Brief 1: SaaS landing page

**Deliverable:** a single self-contained `index.html` in the workspace root.

**Hard constraints (apply to every brief):**
- One HTML file. No build step. No CDN, no external fonts, no external images, no JS libraries.
- System font stack only. Any imagery must be CSS-only (gradients, shapes, SVG you write inline).
- Must be usable at 375px and 1440px wide. No horizontal scrolling at 375px.
- All copy below must appear verbatim. Do not invent testimonials, logos, statistics or prices.
- No lorem ipsum. No placeholder text.

---

**Goal:** a landing page for "Ledgerly", a spend-analytics tool for finance teams at companies of 50–500 people.

**Sections, in order:**
1. Hero — headline, one-sentence subhead, primary CTA "Start free trial", secondary "Book a demo".
2. Three feature blocks, each with a short title and two sentences.
3. A "How it works" strip with three numbered steps.
4. Closing CTA band.

**Fixed copy:**
- H1: "Every card swipe, explained."
- Sub: "Ledgerly categorises company spend automatically, flags duplicates, and closes the month in hours instead of weeks."
- Features: "Auto-categorisation" / "Duplicate detection" / "Month-end close"
- Steps: "Connect your cards" → "Review flagged spend" → "Export to your ledger"
- Closing: "Your books, before the deadline."

**Tone:** confident, plain, no exclamation marks.

---

**When you are done:** write `index.html` and nothing else. Do not explain your choices in the file.
